A 98-second self-narrated film about DeepSeek, told in first person from 2023 to 2026. Every frame, subtitle, voice line and note is generated by code — no stock footage, no samples, no external voice-over. The film is in Chinese with Chinese subtitles.

## How it was made

- **Shots** all come from the `video-shotcraft` recipe cards (124 active cards) — no invented motion.
- **Rendering**: Remotion, frame-by-frame, with **PNG intermediates** (not JPEG) and **1.5× supersampling** to 2880×1620, H.264 CRF 16 + `x264 slow`. The JPEG intermediate was what made earlier cuts look soft.
- **Voice**: `edge-tts` (`zh-CN-YunxiNeural`, +12%). The whole timeline is derived **from the measured duration of each voice line**, so subtitles, cuts and SFX cues share one source of truth.
- **Music**: 120 BPM, all synthesised in code — four-on-the-floor kick, claps, 16th hats, off-beat bass, arpeggio; six rising swooshes on card pulls, with −7.5 dB sidechain ducking under the voice.
